PROJECT 04

Hybrid Solar–Wind Power Generation

Renewable Energy • VAWT • Solar Power • Mechanical Design

Project Overview

The Hybrid Solar–Wind Power Generation System is a renewable energy project combining solar energy and vertical-axis wind turbine technology for sustainable electrical power generation.

The system is designed to utilize both solar and wind energy sources to improve renewable power availability and reduce dependence on a single energy source.

Project Objective

  • Generate electrical power using renewable energy sources.
  • Combine solar and wind power generation.
  • Improve energy availability using a hybrid system.
  • Utilize a Vertical Axis Wind Turbine (VAWT).
  • Develop a sustainable and practical power generation concept.

Working Principle

The solar panel converts sunlight into electrical energy using photovoltaic cells.

The Vertical Axis Wind Turbine converts the kinetic energy of wind into mechanical rotational energy. A DC generator then converts this mechanical energy into electrical energy.

The generated electrical energy from both sources can be managed through a charge controller and stored in a battery. An inverter can be used to convert DC power into AC power for suitable applications.

Major Components

Vertical Axis Wind Turbine

Captures wind energy and converts it into mechanical rotational energy.

Solar Panel

Converts solar radiation into electrical energy using photovoltaic technology.

DC Generator

Converts the mechanical rotation from the wind turbine into electrical energy.

Shaft & Bearings

Support and transmit the rotational motion of the wind turbine system.

Charge Controller

Controls the charging process and manages electrical power supplied to the battery.

Battery

Stores the electrical energy generated by the hybrid renewable energy system.

Inverter

Converts DC electrical power into AC power for suitable electrical applications.

System Configuration

Solar Panel → Charge Controller → Battery → Inverter → Load

Wind Turbine → Shaft & Bearings → DC Generator → Charge Controller → Battery
