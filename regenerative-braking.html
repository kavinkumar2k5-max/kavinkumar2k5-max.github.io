PROJECT 01

Regenerative Braking System

Automotive Engineering • Electric Vehicle • Energy Recovery

Project Overview

The Regenerative Braking System is a conceptual automotive engineering project focused on recovering braking energy and converting it into useful electrical energy.

The system is mainly applicable to electric and hybrid electric vehicles where the kinetic energy normally lost during braking can be partially recovered.

Project Objective

  • Recover energy during vehicle braking.
  • Convert mechanical energy into electrical energy.
  • Improve overall vehicle energy efficiency.
  • Reduce energy losses during braking.
  • Support energy recovery in electric vehicles.

Working Principle

During braking, the traction motor operates as a generator. The rotational energy of the vehicle is converted into electrical energy.

The generated electrical energy can then be supplied to the battery through the vehicle's electrical power management system.

Major Components

Electric Motor

Acts as a motor during acceleration and as a generator during regenerative braking.

Battery Pack

Stores the electrical energy recovered during braking.

Power Electronics

Controls electrical power flow between the motor and battery.

Brake Control System

Coordinates regenerative braking with mechanical braking.

Technical Areas

Automotive Engineering • EV Technology • Energy Recovery • Mechanical Systems
