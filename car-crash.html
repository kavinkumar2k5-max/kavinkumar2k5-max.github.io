PROJECT 03

Car Crash Test Simulation

ANSYS • FEA • Automotive Crash Simulation

Project Overview

The Car Crash Test Simulation is an automotive engineering simulation project focused on studying the structural response of a vehicle during a crash event.

ANSYS-based finite element analysis concepts were used to understand deformation, stress distribution and structural behaviour during impact loading.

Project Objective

  • Analyse vehicle structural behaviour during impact.
  • Study deformation of automotive structures.
  • Understand stress distribution during crash loading.
  • Apply Finite Element Analysis concepts.
  • Improve understanding of automotive crash simulation.

Simulation Approach

The crash simulation process involves creating or importing the vehicle geometry, defining material properties, generating a suitable finite element mesh and applying appropriate impact conditions.

The model can then be analysed to evaluate structural deformation, stress response and other important engineering parameters.

Analysis Parameters

Structural Deformation

Study the deformation of vehicle components during the crash event.

Stress Analysis

Evaluate the stress developed in structural components during impact.

Impact Loading

Analyse the structural response under crash-related impact loading conditions.

Mesh & FEA

Apply finite element meshing and numerical analysis techniques for structural evaluation.
