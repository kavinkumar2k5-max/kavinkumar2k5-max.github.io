PROJECT 05

Sheet Metal Enclosure

CATIA V5 • Sheet Metal Design • Mechanical CAD

Project Overview

The Sheet Metal Enclosure project focuses on the design and development of a sheet metal enclosure for electronic and mechanical applications.

The design was developed with emphasis on dimensions, manufacturability, sheet metal features and proper mechanical assembly.

Project Objective

  • Design a practical sheet metal enclosure.
  • Apply sheet metal modelling techniques.
  • Understand bends, flanges and sheet metal features.
  • Maintain proper dimensions and design accuracy.
  • Develop a manufacturable mechanical enclosure.

Design Approach

The enclosure was developed using sheet metal modelling concepts with consideration for material thickness, bending operations, flange creation and overall component dimensions.

The final design is intended to provide a structured enclosure suitable for protecting and supporting electronic components.

Key Design Features

Sheet Metal Base

Provides the main structural foundation of the enclosure.

Flanges

Used to create the required enclosure walls and improve structural rigidity.

Bend Features

Used to form the enclosure geometry while maintaining proper sheet metal manufacturing requirements.

Assembly Features

Designed to support proper fitting and mechanical assembly of the enclosure.
